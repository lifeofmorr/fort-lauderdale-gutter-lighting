Questions

More about working in Tequesta

My home backs onto the Loxahatchee River — what does riverfront canopy do to gutters?

Riverfront lots carry some of the heaviest canopy in the county — oaks, palms, and pines dropping debris in every season, plus humid river air on the metal. The inspection measures both loads, and the quote specifies a guard system and materials matched to them.

I'm in the Tequesta Country Club community — are there exterior guidelines to plan around?

We review your community's guidelines together before anything is quoted. Roofline lighting tracks mount discreetly under the eaves and read as trim when off, and gutter profiles are chosen to suit the home's architecture.

Can riverfront landscape and dock lighting be part of the same quote process?

Yes — roofline, landscape, and waterfront lighting are planned as one design around your lot and quoted alongside the gutter work or on their own, always starting from the same free inspection.
