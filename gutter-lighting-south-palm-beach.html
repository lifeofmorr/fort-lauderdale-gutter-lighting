South Palm Beach · Residential & Commercial

One quiet strip of A1A, ocean on one side.

South Palm Beach is a narrow barrier-island town between Palm Beach and Manalapan — mostly condominium buildings along A1A, with the Atlantic on one side and the Intracoastal on the other. Salt air is constant, storms come straight off the ocean, and roof edges and drainage on island buildings take the wear.
