A historic small town with working streets.

Mangonia Park is a small town with a long history — incorporated in 1947, set along Australian Avenue just north of West Palm Beach, with a Tri-Rail station, a mix of older single-family homes, and light-industrial streets. The housing stock has decades on it, and many gutter systems are simply due.
