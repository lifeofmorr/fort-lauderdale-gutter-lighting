More about working in Highland Beach

Every home here is between the ocean and the Intracoastal — what does constant salt air do to gutters?

Highland Beach is barely a few blocks wide, so no roofline escapes marine air. Thin sectional metal and cheap fasteners corrode first; the inspection documents the exposure on both faces of your home, and the quote specifies materials chosen for it.

I own a townhome in one of the A1A communities — can my roofline be quoted individually?

Where the roofline is the owner's responsibility, yes — and where the association carries it, the free inspection will tell you that honestly. Either way you get a clear answer about your specific home, at no cost.

Can beach-side lighting be planned around sea-turtle season?

Yes. Highland Beach's beaches are nesting habitat, and ocean-facing lighting has seasonal rules. Fixture choice, shielding, and placement are planned around those requirements before anything is quoted.
