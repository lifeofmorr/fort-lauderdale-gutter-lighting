More about working in Hillsboro Beach

My estate takes salt spray from the ocean on one side and the Intracoastal on the other. What does that mean for gutters?

On this narrow strip of A1A there is no sheltered side of the house — both exposures are marine. The inspection documents corrosion on both faces of the property, and the quote specifies materials and fasteners chosen for full salt exposure on long estate rooflines.

Can roofline and landscape lighting be planned around sea-turtle lighting rules?

Yes. Beachfront properties in Hillsboro Beach sit in sea-turtle nesting territory, and lighting near the sand has to respect seasonal ordinances. We plan fixture type, shielding, and placement around those rules before anything is quoted, so the design you approve is the design that gets installed.

Estates here have guest houses and long compound rooflines — is that one job?

It can be. The free inspection measures the main residence, guest house, and any outbuildings you want covered, and you receive one exact written quote for the whole property.
