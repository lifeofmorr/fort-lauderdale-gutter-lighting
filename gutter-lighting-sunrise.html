Sunrise · Residential & Commercial

From Welleby ranches to Sawgrass rooftops.

Sunrise stretches from the original Leisure Villas and Sunrise Lakes condo communities to the lake homes of Welleby and New River Estates — with Sawgrass Mills and the Amerant Bank Arena drawing the region to its western edge. Much of the city's housing is 1970s and 80s construction: single-story roofs, mature trees, and gutter systems that have quietly aged along with them.
