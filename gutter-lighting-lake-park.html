Lake Park · Residential & Commercial

Kelsey City bones. A hundred years of weather.

Lake Park began as Kelsey City, a planned 1920s community, and its historic district still holds Mediterranean Revival homes and old Florida bungalows from that first building wave — a small, tree-canopied town between US-1 and the Intracoastal. A century of coastal summers sits on these rooflines. Don Morrison is your local contact for gutter protection, seamless gutters, soffit & fascia, and outdoor lighting across Lake Park.
