Such a small village — do you really come out here?

Yes. Lazy Lake sits inside the core service area, between Wilton Manors and Oakland Park — same free inspection, same exact written quote.

Lake humidity hard on gutters?

Constant moisture plus shade is tough on roof edges and metal runs. The inspection checks both and the quote covers what it finds.

Trees over the whole street?

That canopy is the clog source. Protection sheds the debris so the gutters keep working between rains.

More about working in Lazy Lake

Lazy Lake only has a few dozen homes — is my house really in the service area?

Fully. The village is surrounded by Wilton Manors and Oakland Park, both regular service areas, and Lazy Lake homes get the same free inspection and exact written quote.

My home sits right on the lake — does that change what the gutters need?

Shade and moisture mean debris stays damp and roof edges age faster. The inspection looks at the rear roofline and soffit closely, and materials are specified with that exposure in mind.

Can lighting be added without changing the street's quiet feel?

Yes — under-eave tracks are discreet in daylight and the landscape lighting is aimed at your own lot. The quote lays out exactly what would be installed before any work starts.
