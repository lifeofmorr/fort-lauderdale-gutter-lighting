Services in Oakland Park

For Oakland Park's ranch homes and downtown blocks

Gutter protection

Guard systems fitted for the oak and tropical canopy that hangs over the streets near Easterlin Park — debris sheds off instead of settling in, and the ladder stays in the garage.

Seamless gutters

Many Oakland Park homes are mid-century ranches still running original or sectional gutters. Seamless replacements are sized for summer downpours and fitted cleanly to block, stucco, and barrel-tile homes.

Soffit & fascia

On older homes, overflow quietly rots the roof edge for years before it shows inside. Soffit and fascia repair or replacement can be written into the same job as your gutters.

Outdoor lighting

Permanent roofline lighting with discreet under-eave tracks, landscape lighting for oak-shaded yards, and holiday installs — for homes and for downtown storefronts that stay open after dark.
