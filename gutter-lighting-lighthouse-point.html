Guard systems for canal-front rooflines under palms and banyans — fronds and seed pods shed off the surface while heavy rain passes through, even in an inlet thunderstorm.

Replacement runs specified for waterfront exposure, where salt air punishes thin metal and cheap fasteners. Sized for large roof planes and fitted cleanly to stucco and tile-roof homes.

Outdoor lighting
