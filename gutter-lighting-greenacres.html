Greenacres · Residential & Commercial

Old Greenacres, new Greenacres — same afternoon storms.

Greenacres runs from its original 1920s-era street grid near Lake Worth Road — modest single-story homes that have weathered a century of Florida summers — west past Pickwick Park and the Palm Beach State College campus to newer two-story communities like Nautica Isles out by Jog Road. Two very different housing stocks, one shared reality: hard rain, heavy canopy, and gutters that need to work unattended.
