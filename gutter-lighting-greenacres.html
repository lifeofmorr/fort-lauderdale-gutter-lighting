More about working in Greenacres

Greenacres has older east-side homes and brand-new west-side ones — does the quote process differ?

The process is the same free inspection and one exact written quote. What differs is what it finds: original-section homes more often need soffit or fascia attention with their gutters, while newer west-side homes usually need protection fitted to high two-story runs.

My home is near the C-51 canal — does that change anything for my gutters?

Canal-adjacent lots stay humid and collect windblown debris off the water. The inspection checks rear-roofline drainage and roof-edge condition with that in mind, and any added work goes on the same quote.

Can roofline lighting be added to a single-story Greenacres home?

Yes — single-story rooflines are ideal for permanent roofline lighting. The track mounts under the eaves and stays discreet when off, and it can be scoped together with gutter work in one quote.
