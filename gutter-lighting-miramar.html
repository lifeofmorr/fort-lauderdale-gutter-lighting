Questions

More about working in Miramar

My Miramar home is a two-story in a newer community — do I really need gutter protection?

Two-story rooflines are the hardest and riskiest to clean from a ladder, and Miramar's west-side communities sit close to the Everglades edge, where afternoon storms are intense. Guards keep those high runs draining without seasonal cleanouts.

The older side of Miramar has smaller 1960s homes — is the process different there?

The inspection covers the same ground — roofline, existing gutters, soffit and fascia condition — but older homes east of University Drive more often need soffit or fascia attention alongside the gutters, and that can all be written into one quote.

Will permanent roofline lighting work with my community's rules in Monarch Lakes or Silver Falls?

We check your community's exterior guidelines together before quoting. The track mounts under the eaves and reads as a clean trim line when the lights are off, which is what most associations are concerned about.
